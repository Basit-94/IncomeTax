Twelve ways this could look

Every direction shows the same screen with the same figures — the return summary, the one that has to earn trust. Compare on feel, not content. All twelve support light and dark.

Generative & live

The computation is something you watch happen, not a number you are handed.

11

Wash

Your return painted as watercolour. Each pool is one figure, sized by its amount; the green bloom is what comes back. Repaints on demand and re-pigments when you switch theme.

canvas watercolour · granulation · no CDN
Most beautiful
06

Flow

Money moves across the screen as particles — most diverted to tax, a thin stream returning to you. Particle colours read from the live theme.

p5.js · needs a connection
Most memorable
08

Tape

The return computes in front of you, line by line, with a live clock and skeleton rows first. Visible work reads as trustworthy work.

streaming trace · skeletons · replay

Ceremonial

Filing is consequential, so the interface makes it feel that way.

07

Reckoning

A seal draws itself, entries rise as you scroll, and filing takes a press and hold — release early and nothing happens. A stamp lands when it is done.

SVG stroke draw · press-and-hold · stamp
Court / tax office
02

The Chamber

Teak, brass and green baize — a counter you approach and a finding you affirm. Disputes are recorded in place rather than hidden.

seal draw · press-and-hold · dispute log

Document

Trust borrowed from paper. Corrections append a new line and strike the old one — the append-only ledger, made visible.

12

Case File

Your return as a researcher’s pinboard — index cards on graph paper, each with a push-pin, a provenance badge, and a pencil margin note explaining what the figure actually means. Pin all five to unlock filing.

pinboard · margin notes · linked checklist
Best at explaining
01

The Ledger

Ruled accounting paper that draws its own lines. Marking a figure wrong writes a correction beneath it and strikes the original.

rule draw · append-only correction

03

The Receipt

One narrow monospace column that unrolls and prints itself line by line. Impossible to get lost in; reads the same on any phone.

10

Inkline

Rules draw themselves in ink, a brace sweeps around the sum, and confirming an entry pulls a green underline beneath it. Quiet, restrained motion.

Interface

Strata

Pigment

Indigo, haldi, terracotta and leaf — pigments rather than corporate blue, with a drifting jaali pattern. Reads as Indian rather than generic global fintech.

How to choose

You probably want a skin from one and a flow from another. The two ends of your real problem are 03 / 09 (a first-timer needs one column and one decision) and 05 / 08 (a CA needs density and drill-down). Serving both is progressive disclosure, not compromise — so pick a visual language, then borrow the first-time flow and the expert view.

If you want one that people talk about, it is 11 or 06. If you want one a professional signs off on, it is 08 or 05. If you want filing to feel like it matters, it is 07.

Every direction shares the same figures and the same provenance model: each amount names who reported it and when, and every correction is written as a new entry rather than an edit.
