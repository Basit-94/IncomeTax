Employer contribution to the employee's NPS account under s.80CCD(2) is deductible in both regimes, up to 14% of salary under s.115BAC and 10% otherwise (14% for Central and State Government employers). Salary for this purpose means basic pay plus dearness allowance forming part of retirement benefits, not the whole package. Employee contributions fall under s.80CCD(1)/(1B), which the new regime does not allow. Provident fund is not NPS.
