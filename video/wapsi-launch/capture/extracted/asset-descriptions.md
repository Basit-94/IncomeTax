# Asset inventory

No assets were captured. This project runs in **no-capture mode** by explicit user
choice: the video uses invented graphics only — abstract typography, shapes and
motion. No product screenshots, no site crawl, no rebuilt UI.

The only supplied asset is the recorded voiceover:

- `audio/vo.wav` — 7.90s, 48kHz stereo PCM. The finished narration. Verbatim and
  fixed; every visual beat is cut to its word timings (`audio/vo-words.json`).

Brand tokens in `tokens.json` are the product's own design-system values, supplied
from the brief rather than scraped.
