## Intent

A very short launch sting for **Wapsi (वापसी)** — an independent, citizen-first
income-tax filing prototype for India. The narration is already recorded and
fixed: two sentences, 7.9 seconds. Beat one names the pain (filing is
intimidating and confusing). Beat two answers it by name (this is Wapsi, a
citizen-first intelligence platform built for the people of India).

Tone: calm and dignified, not startup-hypey. The product's whole thesis is that
a citizen deserves to understand where their own money is, so the video should
feel like relief arriving — clarity landing on top of confusion — rather than a
feature parade.

## Assets

- audio/vo.wav — the finished voiceover, 7.9s, 48kHz stereo. Verbatim and fixed; every visual beat cuts to it.

## Customizations

- **No-capture mode.** The user explicitly chose invented graphics only: abstract
  typography, shapes and motion. Do NOT screen-capture or rebuild the product UI,
  and do not imply a shipped product by mocking up screens.
- The word "Wapsi" (and वापसी) is the payoff of the piece — it should land as the
  visual resolution at ~4.1s, when the narration turns from problem to answer.

## Notes

- 7.9s is far below this route's 30–90s sweet spot. That is intentional and
  fixed by the recorded audio — the cut must match the VO exactly, not stretch.
- Two beats only: 0.00–4.08s problem, 4.08–7.90s answer. Do not invent a third.
- The name is Wapsi — Hindi for "return / coming back", the pun the product is
  built on. Whisper mis-transcribes it as "Papsi"; never use that spelling.
- This is an independent prototype, NOT affiliated with the Income Tax
  Department, CBDT, or the Government of India. Avoid any government seal,
  emblem, tricolour-as-officialdom, or wording that implies official status.
